World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026. Millions of Spanish workers staged the country's first general strike in 14 years over housing costs, backed by unions UGT and CCOO, 18 days before a snap election on 29 November (Reuters). Russia's ambassador to the US, Alexander Darchiev, died suddenly in Moscow aged 66, the Russian Foreign Ministry said (Reuters). Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to being declared Ebola-free after nine cases, seven recoveries and two deaths (Reuters). Iran designated Argentina's navy and air force as terrorist organisations, its Foreign Ministry said (Just Security). In Paris, President Emmanuel Macron led Armistice Day commemorations for the last time as president, six months before France's 2027 election (Le Monde).
